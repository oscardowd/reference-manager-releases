            Citation style Applies to every citation and the bibliography in this document.

            Bibliography Inserted at the cursor. Put the cursor where the list should go, usually the end of the document.
            Update citations Re-formats every citation and the bibliography, and renumbers numeric styles.

            Desktop app This pane reads your library from Reference Manager on this computer. Keep it open while you write.

            Working with citations Tick references to group them in one citation. Open a selected reference for page numbers, a prefix or a suffix.
            • Open the search with ⌘⌥R on Mac or Ctrl Alt R on Windows.
            • To change a citation, click it in the document, then choose Edit citation at cursor.
            • After deleting or moving citations, choose Update document on the Document tab.
