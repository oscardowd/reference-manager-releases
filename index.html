Reference Manager

Cite as you write in Microsoft Word, and keep your library on your own computer. Free, with no account.

Download

  • Mac — Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 12 or later: Reference Manager 1.0.0. Signed and notarised by Apple. Open the zip, move Reference Manager to your Applications folder, and open it. It updates itself, and asks before it restarts.
  • Windows — coming soon.

In Word

The Reference Manager add-in works in Word for Mac, Windows, iPad and the web. On its own it works from the references in your document, and adds new ones by DOI, PubMed ID or by hand. With the desktop app open, it searches your whole library.

  • With the desktop app: choose Word › Set Up Word on This Mac. It adds the add-in to Word and makes a secure connection that only your computer can use.
  • From Microsoft's add-in store: coming soon.
